This data pack contains sample GIS files for use in a demonstration exercise as part of the workshop "SunoikisisDC Digital Approaches to Cultural Heritage: Session 5 | Geographic Information Systems (https://github.com/SunoikisisDC/SunoikisisDC-2025-2026/wiki/5-GIS). The files are not intended for distribution. If you would like to use these data in your work, you should check with the original publishers to make sure you are abiding by their licensing requirements.

1. ClippedDEM.tif - Digital elevation model (DEM) raster. The extent of this raster is clipped from its original, and reprojected to use the EPSG:32637 - WGS84/UTM zone 37N.

Source: National Aeronautics and Space Administration's (NASA) Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM), surveyed 11 Feb 2000 to 22 Feb 2000, SRTM GL1, 1 arc-second (~30m spatial resolution)

2. PointData
	i. Book5_visited.shp - Vector point data for visited settlements in Book 5 of Xenophon's Anabasis. These data are a small part of Tracking the Ten Thousand, an (as of writing) ongoing project by Cian Colgan at Furman University. Point data is modified from Pleiades: A Gazetteer of Past Places.
	ii. Book5_ambiguous.shp - Vector point data for locations tagged as ambiguous in Book 5 of Xenophon's Anabasis. These data are a small part of Tracking the Ten Thousand, an (as of writing) ongoing project by Cian Colgan at Furman University. Point data is modified from Pleiades: A Gazetteer of Past Places.
